# Mathematics topics

This is the working topic map for our middle-school Science Bowl mathematics preparation. It consolidates the concepts found in the DOE past-exam archive's Math and Mathematics question categories.

## Lecture-note standard

Each lecture note should cover both **what the topic is** and **how to solve its questions quickly**. Include:

- Core definitions, formulas, and a short worked example
- Fast methods or useful shortcuts appropriate for timed Science Bowl play
- Recognition cues: what wording or structure signals the technique to use
- Common traps, including tempting but incorrect shortcuts
- A few increasingly difficult practice problems with concise solutions

## Arithmetic and number sense

- Whole-number operations and signed numbers
- Fractions, mixed numbers, decimals, and order of operations
- Rounding, estimation, place value, and scientific notation
- Absolute value and reciprocals
- Ratios, proportions, rates, and unit rates
- Percentages, percent change, discounts, tax, and interest
- Measurement and unit conversions

## Number theory

- Primes, composites, factors, multiples, and divisibility
- Greatest common factor and least common multiple
- Remainders and modular arithmetic
- Parity, integers, rational and irrational numbers
- Number bases and digit problems
- Factorials, consecutive numbers, and Fibonacci-style patterns

## Algebra and functions

- Expressions, variables, constants, coefficients, and terms
- Simplifying, evaluating, expanding, and factoring expressions
- Linear equations, inequalities, and systems of equations
- Exponents, powers, roots, radicals, and logarithms
- Polynomials, degrees, binomial/trinomial expansion, and quadratics
- Functions, sequences, series, and graphs
- Matrices, determinants, and introductory complex numbers

## Coordinate geometry

- Ordered pairs, quadrants, intercepts, and the coordinate plane
- Slope and slope-intercept form
- Distance and midpoint
- Parallel and perpendicular lines
- Coordinate transformations

## Plane geometry

- Angle relationships, including complementary and supplementary angles
- Triangles, congruence, similarity, and the Pythagorean relationship
- Polygons, quadrilaterals, interior/exterior angles, and symmetry
- Perimeter, area, and scale factors

## Circle geometry and trigonometry

- Radius, diameter, circumference, arcs, chords, and sectors
- Tangents, secants, and inscribed figures
- Degrees, radians, and introductory sine, cosine, and tangent

## Solid geometry

- Nets, prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones, and spheres
- Surface area and volume
- Space diagonals, faces, and edges

## Probability, counting, and statistics

- Probability and sample outcomes
- Counting principles, permutations, combinations, and arrangements
- Mean, median, mode, range, and weighted averages
- Tables, histograms, scatter plots, and introductory standard deviation

## Sets, logic, and applied problems

- Sets, subsets, union, intersection, complement, and Venn diagrams
- Basic logical and truth-statement questions
- Distance-rate-time, work, age, mixture, money, clock, calendar, travel, and multi-step word problems

## Scope note

This map reflects questions categorized by DOE as **Math** or **Mathematics**. It does not attempt to include quantitative questions assigned to Physics, Chemistry, Energy, or other Science Bowl categories.
