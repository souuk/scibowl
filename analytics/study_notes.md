# Study notes

## How to use this collection

1. Work a round under timed conditions, then review answers immediately.
2. Use `topic_distribution.md` to select a category you want to emphasize.
3. Use `pdf_inventory.md` to locate rounds containing a category label.
4. Cross-check older factual answers, especially science/energy facts that may have changed since publication.

## Interpreting labels

The collection mixes historical and newer terminology. In particular, `Life Science` and `Biology`, `Earth and Space` and `Earth Science`, and `Math` and `Mathematics` can describe closely related competition areas. The reports preserve the original detected wording so the historical structure remains visible.
